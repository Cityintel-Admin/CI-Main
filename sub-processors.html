CityIntel gives clients reasonable advance notice before any new sub-processor begins processing their personal data, in accordance with the Data Processing Agreement. Recent changes are listed at the foot of this page.

CityIntel also retrieves data from a number of public or commercial intelligence feeds to populate live dashboards — transport disruptions, power outages, flight and airspace status, and news.
